Focus areas

Three domains. One wayof working.

In each, we help with data, building the solution, evaluation and design — then work with teams to take what proves out beyond the pilot.

01

Agriculture × AI

We work with organisations using data and AI to improve agriculture and address the challenges affecting farmers, food systems, productivity, resilience and livelihoods. Our role is to strengthen the data and evidence underneath the solution, and to help the teams building it make better decisions about what to build.

02

Health × AI

We support organisations developing data and AI solutions for health and healthcare challenges. We don't provide medical care — we help the organisations that do, with the research, data and evaluation capacity that tells them whether a solution is working and how to improve it.

DataResearchAI solution developmentEvaluationDesignEvidence generationScaling
03

Education × AI

We support organisations using data and AI to improve education and address challenges in learning and access — building around the realities of the classrooms and systems they serve rather than assuming ideal conditions.

Scaling solutions

Helping effectivesolutions reach thepeople who needthem most.

Most good solutions stall after the pilot. We work with the teams building them to connect promising, evidence-backed work with INGOs, governments, development organisations, funders and implementation partners looking to adopt what has been shown to work.

We make introductions, build the evidence case, and support the process. We don't guarantee adoption or funding — that decision always sits with the institution.
